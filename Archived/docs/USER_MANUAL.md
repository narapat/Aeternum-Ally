# User Manual

## Welcome to AeternumAlly

AeternumAlly guides your organization through a structured sustainability reporting workflow. By the end of the process, you will have a draft Sustainability Statement aligned with ESRS (European Sustainability Reporting Standards) and cross-referenced to GRI standards — without needing prior expertise in sustainability reporting.

This manual covers every screen and feature in plain language. Follow the sections in order the first time; return to individual sections as a reference when needed.

## 1. Getting Started

### Creating your account

AeternumAlly uses email magic-links — there is no password to create or remember.

1. Go to the app URL and enter your email address on the sign-in screen.
2. Check your inbox for an email with a sign-in link.
3. Click the link. You will be taken directly into the app.

The link is valid for a limited time. If it has expired, return to the sign-in page and request a new one.

### Setting up your organization

The first time you sign in, you will be asked to set up an organization.

- If your company is new to the platform, click **Create Organization**. You become the Owner of the new organization.
- If a colleague has invited you, click the link in your invitation email instead. You will be added automatically to their organization.

## 2. Navigating the App

The left sidebar contains the main navigation. Each item corresponds to a section of the workflow:

| Sidebar item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Overview | Data Completeness Dashboard — see your progress at a glance |
| Company Profile | Enter your company's basic information |
| Business Model Canvas | Map your value creation and sustainability model |
| Materiality Assessment | Score ESRS topics for impact and financial materiality |
| Materiality Matrix | Visualize which topics are material |

Dark mode is available via the sun/moon icon in the sidebar. Your preference is saved on this device.

## 3. Company Profile

This section captures your organization's identity. The information you enter here is used throughout the app to personalize AI suggestions.

### Fields to complete

**Basic identity**
- Company name
- Tax / registration number
- Industry sector (choose from the dropdown list)
- ISIC code (an international industry classification — select the code that best fits your main activity)
- Founding year
- Website
- Address

**Size**
- Employee count: choose Micro (under 10), Small (10–49), or Medium (50–249)
- Revenue range: choose the band that applies to your last financial year

**Description**
- General description: Write 2–5 sentences about what your company does, who it serves, and how. This is the most important field — AI features draw on this text heavily.
- Mission: Your company's purpose statement
- Vision: Where you aim to be in the long term
- Products / services: A comma-separated list of your main offerings

### Saving

The profile saves automatically when you move between fields. You will see a small save indicator in the top-right corner. You can also click **Save** to save immediately.

## 4. Business Model Canvas

The canvas maps how your company creates, delivers, and captures value — extended to include environmental and social dimensions.

### The eleven blocks

Work through each block by clicking on it and typing in the text area:

**Core business blocks**
- **Key Partners:** Who do you rely on? (suppliers, distributors, technology providers)
- **Key Activities:** What does your company actually do to deliver your value? (manufacturing, software development, service delivery)
- **Key Resources:** What assets make this possible? (equipment, IP, team expertise, facilities)
- **Value Proposition:** What problem do you solve? What benefit do you provide?
- **Customer Relationships:** How do you attract and keep customers?
- **Channels:** How do you reach your customers? (online, retail, direct sales)
- **Customer Segments:** Who are your customers? (demographics, industries, geographies)
- **Cost Structure:** What are your main costs?
- **Revenue Streams:** How do you make money?

**Sustainability extensions**
- **Eco-Social Costs:** What negative impacts does your business have on the environment or society? (emissions, waste, labor conditions in your supply chain)
- **Eco-Social Benefits:** What positive contributions do you make? (carbon reduction, local job creation, fair wages)

### Using AI suggestions

Each block has a small sparkle or wand icon. Click it to ask the AI to draft content for that block based on your company description. You can accept the suggestion as-is, edit it, or discard it and write your own text.

### Saving

The canvas auto-saves as you type. No manual save required.

## 5. Double Materiality Assessment

This is the analytical core of the platform. You will evaluate up to ten ESRS topics and decide which ones are "material" — significant enough to require disclosure in your sustainability report.

### Understanding materiality

Each topic is scored on two dimensions:

**Impact Materiality (Inside-Out):** How significant is your company's impact on people and the environment through this topic?

**Financial Materiality (Outside-In):** How significantly does this topic affect your company's financial performance — through risks or opportunities?

A topic is material if it scores above the threshold on either dimension.

### Creating an assessment

1. Click **Add Assessment** (the + button).
2. Select an ESRS topic from the dropdown. The ten topics are:
   - E1 Climate Change
   - E2 Pollution
   - E3 Water & Marine Resources
   - E4 Biodiversity & Ecosystems
   - E5 Resource Use & Circular Economy
   - S1 Own Workforce
   - S2 Workers in the Value Chain
   - S3 Affected Communities
   - S4 Consumers & End-users
   - G1 Business Conduct

3. Write (or generate) an impact description: describe the specific ways your company affects people or the environment through this topic.

4. Write (or generate) a financial description: describe the risks and opportunities this topic creates for your financial performance.

5. Score the Impact Materiality criteria:
   - **Scale:** How severe is the impact? (1 = Minimal, 5 = Critical)
   - **Scope:** How widespread is it? (1 = Minimal, 5 = Critical)
   - **Irremediability:** How hard is it to reverse? (1 = Minimal, 5 = Critical)
   - **Likelihood:** How likely is it to occur? (1 = Very Unlikely, 5 = Certain)

6. Score the Financial Materiality criteria:
   - **Magnitude:** How large is the financial impact? (1 = Minimal, 5 = Critical)
   - **Likelihood:** How likely is it? (1 = Very Unlikely, 5 = Certain)

The system calculates your score automatically and shows you whether the topic is Material or Not Material.

7. Click **Save**.

### Using Auto-Fill

Click **Auto-Fill** to have the AI draft the impact and financial descriptions and suggest scores based on your company profile and the selected topic. Review each suggestion carefully — AI output reflects general industry knowledge and may not capture your specific situation. Adjust as needed before saving.

### Editing and deleting assessments

Click any existing assessment in the list to open and edit it. Use the delete icon to remove an assessment you no longer need.

## 6. Materiality Matrix

The matrix is a scatter plot that shows all your assessed topics as dots. The position of each dot reflects its scores:

- Left-right axis: Financial Materiality (0–100)
- Up-down axis: Impact Materiality (0–100)
- Dashed lines: the materiality threshold at 40 on each axis

Topics appearing above or to the right of the threshold lines are flagged as material. Topics in the lower-left quadrant are not material under the current scores.

Hover over a dot to see the topic name and exact scores. The matrix updates automatically whenever you add or edit an assessment.

Use this view to sense-check your scores: if a topic you consider business-critical appears in the non-material zone, revisit its scoring.

## 7. SWOT Analysis

The SWOT wizard takes you through four quadrants one at a time:

**Step 1 — Strengths:** What does your organization do well? What advantages do you have?

**Step 2 — Weaknesses:** Where do you have gaps or vulnerabilities?

**Step 3 — Opportunities:** What external trends or market shifts could you benefit from?

**Step 4 — Threats:** What external risks, regulatory changes, or competitive pressures could harm you?

### Using AI Search Grounding

In the Opportunities and Threats steps, click **Search & Suggest**. The AI performs a live Google Search for news, regulatory updates, and market trends relevant to your industry and geography, then drafts a list of relevant points for you to review and edit. This is especially useful for the Threats section, where keeping up with regulation changes is time-consuming.

Accept, modify, or replace the AI suggestions before clicking **Next**.

### Saving

The SWOT analysis auto-saves as you complete each step.

## 8. Performance Dashboard (KPIs)

Track your sustainability initiatives using the Balanced Scorecard framework, which organizes performance across four perspectives:

- **Financial:** Revenue impact, cost savings, fines avoided
- **Customer:** Customer satisfaction, retention, complaints
- **Internal Processes:** Operational targets, emissions reductions, waste reduction
- **Learning & Growth:** Employee training, innovation, capability building

### Creating a KPI

Click **Add KPI** and fill in:

- **Name:** A short, clear label (e.g., "Carbon emissions per unit")
- **Description:** Full definition of what is being measured
- **Perspective:** Choose the BSC quadrant
- **Frequency:** Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually
- **Unit:** The measurement unit (%, tCO2e, THB, number of incidents, etc.)
- **Target value:** The goal you are aiming for
- **Current value:** Your current performance
- **Linked KPIs:** Other KPIs this one influences (optional, for causal mapping)
- **RACI:**
  - Responsible: who does the work
  - Accountable: who owns the result (usually a manager or director)
  - Consulted: who provides input
  - Informed: who needs to know the outcome
- **History:** Log past data points to generate trend lines

### Using AI KPI Suggestions

Click **Suggest KPIs** to have the AI propose a set of KPIs based on your company description and the material topics from your assessment. Review each suggestion and click the checkmark on the ones you want to add.

### Viewing progress

Each KPI shows a progress bar comparing current value to target. Click a KPI to see its history as a trend chart. Filter the dashboard by perspective, frequency, or owner using the filter controls at the top.

## 9. Data Completeness Dashboard

The Overview screen (the home screen) shows a completeness summary for every section:

- Company Profile: how many required fields are filled
- Business Model Canvas: how many blocks have content
- Materiality Assessments: how many of the 10 topics have been assessed
- SWOT Analysis: how many quadrants are complete
- KPIs: how many KPIs have been defined

Use this dashboard to identify gaps before generating your Sustainability Statement. A statement can be generated at any completeness level, but a more complete dataset produces a richer document.

## 10. Sustainability Statement

### Generating the statement

When you are ready, click **Generate Statement** from the Sustainability Statement screen. The system collects all your data and sends it to the AI to produce a structured draft report.

Generation takes between 10 and 60 seconds depending on the volume of your data.

### What the statement contains

The generated document follows ESRS 2 structure:

**General Disclosures (ESRS 2)**
- Company overview
- Governance approach to sustainability
- Strategy and business model description
- Double materiality process explanation

**Topical disclosures**
One section per material topic (E1, E2, S1, etc.), covering:
- Description of the impact, risk, or opportunity
- Actions taken or planned
- Relevant data and metrics

**GRI Content Index**
A table at the end mapping each ESRS topic to the equivalent GRI standards, making the statement compatible with GRI-based reporting requests from customers or investors.

### Reviewing and editing

The statement is a draft for your review, not a final audited disclosure. Read through the content carefully. Any factual details drawn from AI should be verified against your actual data.

Currently, editing is done by updating the underlying data (profile, assessments, KPIs) and regenerating. Direct in-line editing of the statement text is not available in this version.

### Exporting to PDF

Click **Export to PDF** or use your browser's print function (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P). Select "Save as PDF" as the printer destination. The layout is optimized for print.

## 11. Team Management

Team management is accessible from the organization settings panel (gear icon or your profile menu).

### Inviting a team member

1. Enter the person's email address.
2. Select their role:
   - **Admin:** Full access, can invite and manage members
   - **Manager:** Full access to sustainability data, cannot manage members
   - **Consultant:** Read-only access
3. Click **Send Invite**.

The person will receive an email with a link to join. Links expire after 7 days.

### Resending an expired invite

If an invitee's link has expired, they can request a new one from the sign-in page by entering their email address and clicking **Resend Invite Link**.

### Removing a member

Only Owners and Admins can remove members. Click the member's row in the team list and select **Remove**. The action is immediate.

### Viewing your role

Your current role is shown in the sidebar under your name. If you believe your role is incorrect, contact your organization's Owner.

## 12. AI Usage Panel

Owners and Admins can view the AI Usage Panel from the sidebar or settings menu.

The panel shows:

- Date and time of each AI call
- Which feature triggered the call (e.g., "IRO suggestions", "sustainability statement", "SWOT threats")
- The AI model used
- Number of tokens consumed (input and output)

Use this panel to understand your AI consumption, especially if you are self-hosting and managing your own Gemini API quota.

## 14. Troubleshooting

### The sign-in link did not arrive

- Check your spam or junk folder.
- Wait 2–3 minutes; email delivery can be delayed.
- Return to the sign-in page and request a new link.
- If using a corporate email, ask your IT team to whitelist the sender domain.

### AI features are not responding

- Ensure you are signed in and have an active organization.
- Check your internet connection.
- If you are using the live demo, the shared AI quota may be exhausted during peak hours. Try again later or self-host with your own Gemini API key.
- If self-hosting, verify that `GEMINI_API_KEY` is correctly set in your Netlify environment variables.

### My invite link has expired

Go to the sign-in page, enter your email, and click **Resend Invite Link**. You will receive a fresh link within a few minutes.

### Data is not saving

- Check the save indicator in the top-right corner. A spinning icon means a save is in progress.
- Check your internet connection.
- If the problem persists, refresh the page. Auto-saved data will be reloaded from the database.

### The materiality matrix is empty

The matrix only displays topics that have been assessed. Go to the Double Materiality Assessment section and add at least one assessment record.

### The Sustainability Statement generation failed

- Ensure the Company Profile (especially the description field) is filled in.
- Ensure at least one topic has been assessed and marked material.
- Check your internet connection.
- Try again after a short wait. If the Gemini API is rate-limiting requests, a retry after 30–60 seconds usually succeeds.

### I cannot see a section in the sidebar

Some features are role-restricted. If you are a Consultant, you have read-only access and some management actions will not be visible. Contact your organization's Owner to request a role change.
