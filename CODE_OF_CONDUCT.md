# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Aeternum Ally a respectful and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

---

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in all communications
- Welcome newcomers and help them get started
- Give and accept constructive feedback graciously
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not on people
- Acknowledge others' contributions

---

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments of any kind
- Personal attacks or public shaming
- Sharing others' private information without consent
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel where contributors interact on behalf of this project.

---

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to **conduct@aeternumally.com**. All reports will be reviewed confidentially. Maintainers reserve the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions and to ban contributors who violate this code of conduct.

---

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
